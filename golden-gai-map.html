新宿ゴールデン街

Six narrow alleys in Shinjuku, packed with 200+ tiny bars that seat five to ten people each. Point at a bar to peek inside. Click or tap it for cover charge, hours and Instagram.

    Map labels

    Most signboards are in Japanese. Switch to 日本語 to match what you see on the street.

    Alleys

      Each bar is colored by the alley its door opens onto. Tap an alley to highlight its bars.

      Street level Upstairs Basement

      Before you go

      • Most bars add a seating fee (cover charge) on top of your drinks. Check it before you sit down.
      • Some bars are for regulars only. Look for signs welcoming first-timers.
      • Many bars ask guests not to take photos. Ask the staff first.
      • Bring cash. Not every bar takes cards.
